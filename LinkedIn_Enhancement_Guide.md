## 4. PROJECTS & PORTFOLIO

### Add Featured Projects
1. Click "Projects" in the "Additional" section
2. Add 5-7 of your best projects with:
   - Project title
   - Description (100-500 chars)
   - Start/End date
   - Link to GitHub or live site
   - Associated skills

## 5. RECOMMENDATIONS & ENDORSEMENTS

### Getting Recommendations
- ✅ Ask 3-5 people for recommendations
- ✅ Request from: colleagues, managers, clients, teammates
- ✅ Reciprocate: write recommendations for others
- ✅ Recommendations are LinkedIn's "social proof"

**Template for requesting:**
```
Subject: Could you write me a LinkedIn recommendation?

Hi [Name],

I've really enjoyed working with you on [project/time period]. 
Your feedback was invaluable and I'd appreciate a brief LinkedIn 
recommendation if you're willing. I'm happy to write one for you in return!

Thanks!
```

### Endorsements Strategy
- ✅ Ask people to endorse your top 3-5 skills
- ✅ Endorse others first to prompt reciprocation
- ✅ Aim for 20+ endorsements per skill
