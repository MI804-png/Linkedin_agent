About the job

Overview:

Our client, a high-growth fintech infrastructure company, is looking for Full-Stack Software Engineers to help build the systems powering modern money movement. This is an opportunity to work on APIs, banking infrastructure, and financial workflows that enable businesses of all sizes to move money globally.

Unlike traditional SaaS platforms that move data, our client is building systems that move money — where scale, accuracy, resilience and reliability are critical. This role is suited to engineers excited by complex distributed systems, thoughtful API design, and solving hard problems in payments infrastructure.

What you’ll be doing:

Build end-to-end product features across backend and frontend systems

Develop APIs and core infrastructure that support high-volume money movement

Contribute to systems focused on scalability, fault tolerance and long-term maintainability

Work closely with product and engineering teams to shape projects from concept through delivery

Take ownership of core platform components while shipping code frequently

Grow into a mentorship role within a strong engineering culture

Tech Environment:

Open to engineers from any modern stack including Ruby, Python, Go, TypeScript/Node, Java or Kotlin — strong backend fundamentals, distributed systems experience and API/infrastructure expertise matter more than any specific language.

What they’re looking for:

2+ years of professional software engineering experience

Interest in full-stack development across backend systems, databases and frontend UX

Track record of collaborating to deliver meaningful product or platform features

Experience building with modern frameworks and scalable backend systems

Exposure to payments, banking infrastructure, card processing, crypto or financial systems is highly valued

Comfortable in an autonomous, engineering-led environment

Why it’s compelling:

Work at the heart of payments and financial infrastructure

Solve complex technical challenges in money movement and distributed systems

Join a high-caliber engineering team with strong ownership and mentorship culture

Opportunity to work at the intersection of traditional payments and emerging stablecoin infrastructure

Significant scope to shape product and platform at scale

If you're excited by building critical infrastructure that powers the future of payments, we’d love to share more.
