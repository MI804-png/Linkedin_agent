Meet the Team

Cisco IT is a transformation engine enabling people to do the best work of their lives! Our vision is to be loved as the world’s best IT organization. We lead with user experience, accept agile ways of working, drive engineering efficiency, and deliver with Cloud first, shift left, AI powered technology.

The Component Operations Supply Chain IT team is at the forefront of transforming our supply chain through technology. We specialize in implementing and optimizing our Supply Chain ERP solution, a critical backbone for managing everything from procurement to delivery. Our expertise ensures seamless integration of processes, providing real-time visibility and control over our entire supply chain operations. We empower data-driven decisions that enhance efficiency, reduce costs, and improve overall operational agility.

What you'll do:

As a OIC engineer you will collaborate with a team of skilled engineers and interact with business partners. You will plan, direct, and coordinate central initiatives for the Component Operations Supply Chain IT org.

  • Responsible for development, test, and deployment of robust, scalable integrations between Oracle Fusion Cloud and other internal/external systems using Oracle Integration Cloud (OIC).
  • Monitor and debug integrations, resolve performance issues, and provide technical guidance.
  • Collaborate with architects and stakeholders to gather requirements, understand business processes, and translate them into effective technical solutions.
  • Provide ongoing technical support including troubleshooting production issues, identifying root causes, and implementing timely resolutions.
  • Participate in all phases of the software development lifecycle, including requirements analysis, design, coding, testing, deployment, and support operations.
  • Write and maintain clear, concise, and comprehensive technical documentation for all developed solutions, integrations, and processes.
  • Responsible for code and design reviews while managing code versioning and deployments across different environments (Development, Test, Production) using best practices for change management.

Minimum Qualifications:

  • Bachelor’s degree in computer science, IT or related field with 5-7 years of Oracle technology experience.
  • Strong expertise in Oracle Integration Cloud (OIC) implementations and developing complex integrations (scheduled, app-driven, event-based) handling large volumes.
  • Proficient in REST, SOAP web services, XML, JSON, XSLT, and integrating with external systems, databases, and SFTP/FTP.
  • Skilled in error handling, exception management, logging, security features
  • Experience applying AI to technology solutions.

Preferred Qualifications :

  • Experience in developing web applications using VBCS.Consume and integrate REST APIs and external services, Oracle BI Publisher Reports
  • Python, Javascript, SQL and PLSQL
  • Prior experience working with Oracle Fusion Cloud modules, including ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning), SCM (Supply Chain Management), and CX (Customer Experience).
